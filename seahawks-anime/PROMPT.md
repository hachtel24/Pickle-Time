# THE TWELFTH AWAKENS — Seahawks Anime Prompt (revised)

Create a 2D anime scene for the web. It must look like real Japanese theatrical anime: traditional-looking, hand-drawn 2D, not 3D, not a moving illustration, not a stylized web graphic. Deliver it as a single self-contained HTML file. Any tools or techniques are allowed as long as the final result reads as hand-drawn 2D anime.

Use Studio Ghibli films as the reference for design, line work, painted backgrounds, color, and atmosphere. The main character is an original giant robot built in Seattle Seahawks colors: **college navy, action green, and wolf grey**. Its helmet has a hawk-like brow and swept-back crest feathers. A faded stenciled **"12"** is painted on its chest plate. Fans call it **The Twelfth**. No people appear in the scene, and no real team logos are used.

## Scene — Pacific Northwest, sunrise on game day
Mist hangs over an abandoned old-growth forest of Douglas firs and ferns. Mount Rainier glows pink on the horizon. The Twelfth has knelt here for so long, waiting for a kickoff that never came, that moss, lupine, wildflowers, and a young birch have grown over its body. A young **osprey** (the real-world seahawk) lands on its shoulder pauldron. The first ray of sunlight reaches its weathered hawk-browed face. Deep inside its chest, behind the faded 12, an **action-green** light flickers on.

The ground trembles and birds burst from the treetops, like a stadium crowd rising to its feet. With a deep, creaking groan, the robot slowly rises, and soil, vines, and moss pour from its armor. It lifts its head toward a gap in the canopy, and its eyes ignite green. The thrusters fire and send a great ring of wind across the forest, like the roar of 68,000 fans. The robot tears into the golden sky, leaving a curving trail of light and mist that arcs toward the distant Seattle skyline, while leaves spiral down through the sunbeams.

End card: **THE TWELFTH AWAKENS**, with the subtitle 十二の目覚め.

## Anime quality standard
- **Robot:** heavy, ancient, full of personality; cel-colored forms, chipped navy and grey paint, rust, moss; on-model in every shot. Posture and glowing green eyes carry the emotion.
- **Line art:** confident hand-inked lines, thicker on the shadow side, tapered strokes.
- **Coloring:** traditional cel coloring with hard-edged violet shadows and warm sunrise rim light.
- **Backgrounds:** gouache-style painted backgrounds with visible brushwork, layered firs, real depth, and no outlines.
- **Lighting:** golden sunrise, colored shadows, glowing mist, god rays.
- **Animation:** animated on 2s (12 drawings/sec) for the robot and on 1s for effects, with strong key poses, holds, anticipation, overlapping vines and leaves, a slow, heavy rise, and an explosive launch.
- **Direction:** cinematic cuts: establishing wide, the osprey close-up, the chest-light insert, the tremor wide, a low-angle rise, the eye-ignite close-up, the wind ring, and the sky launch.

## It must not look like
3D/CGI · flat vector art · web-animation sliding/bobbing · AI-warped art · Western cartoon · amateur stiffness, empty backgrounds or repeated trees.

Inspect frames as stills and keep improving until each one holds up as a theatrical anime still.
